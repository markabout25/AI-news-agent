# AI Research Digest — November 15, 2026

## Key Themes
The AI world is reeling from the sudden death of Jensen Huang, NVIDIA's co-founder and CEO, with analysts and the open-source community grappling with what this means for GPU supply chains, NVIDIA's strategic direction, and the broader AI ecosystem. Amid the upheaval, technical research continues apace, with new deep dives into foundational LLM architecture components like positional embeddings.

## Takeaway
The death of Jensen Huang is the most consequential leadership event in AI since the field's current boom began — his singular vision unified hardware strategy, developer ecosystem, and open-source enablement at NVIDIA, and the vacuum he leaves will reshape competitive dynamics, supply chains, and the open-source AI movement for years to come.
