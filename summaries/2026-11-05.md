# AI Industry Digest — November 5, 2026

## Key Themes
The AI industry is confronting a critical infrastructure bottleneck as political opposition to data center construction gains real electoral traction. Meanwhile, incumbents like Intuit are navigating the AI paradigm shift, and researchers are working to quantify the rapidly growing open-source model ecosystem.

## Top Stories

1. **[AI on the Ballot](https://stratechery.com/2026/ai-on-the-ballot/)** — *Stratechery* — Nov 4, 2026
Data center proposals suffered significant defeats at the ballot box, raising serious questions about whether America's "build nothing" political culture will become a permanent constraint on AI infrastructure. This could reshape where compute gets built globally and who controls the next phase of AI scaling.

2. **[An Interview With Intuit CEO Sasan Goodarzi About Accounting and AI](https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-intuit-ceo-sasan-goodarzi-about-accounting-and-ai/)** — *Stratechery* — Nov 5, 2026
Intuit's CEO discusses how one of the oldest software companies in tech is repositioning around AI, offering a window into how established enterprise players are adapting rather than being displaced. The accounting and tax domain represents a high-stakes test case for AI augmenting — or replacing — professional knowledge work.

3. **[How big is the open model economy today?](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/how-well-can-we-measure-open-model)** — *Interconnects* — Nov 4, 2026
Nathan Lambert's team releases research quantifying the size and shape of the open model economy, a metric that has been surprisingly difficult to pin down. This work matters as the open vs. closed model debate increasingly drives policy, investment, and strategic decisions across the industry.

## Takeaway
The electoral rejection of data centers is arguably the most consequential development this cycle: if the US cannot build the physical infrastructure AI requires, it doesn't matter how good the models get — the scaling roadmap breaks, and the geopolitical center of gravity for AI compute shifts elsewhere.
