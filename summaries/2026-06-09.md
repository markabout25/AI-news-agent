## Top Stories

1. **[The iPhone's Last Stand](https://stratechery.com/2026/the-iphones-last-stand/)** — *Stratechery, June 9*
Ben Thompson argues that Apple's Siri doesn't need to be the best AI assistant — it just needs to be good enough. This frames the AI assistant race as one decided by ecosystem leverage, not model benchmarks.
