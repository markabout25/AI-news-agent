# AI Digest — May 4–5, 2026

## Key Themes
The AI industry is bifurcating between companies that can **monetize AI investments now** versus those still spending ahead of revenue — and Wall Street is picking winners accordingly. Meanwhile, DeepSeek continues to close the frontier gap, reigniting debates around distillation, open models, and competitive moats. The legal and corporate maneuvering around OpenAI (Musk lawsuit, Microsoft/Amazon deals) remains a significant sideshow.

## Top Stories

1. **[Google Earnings, Meta Earnings](https://stratechery.com/2026/google-earnings-meta-earnings/)** — *Stratechery, May 4*
Wall Street rewarded Google and punished Meta despite Meta's arguably stronger core business performance. The key divergence: Google is already monetizing its AI investments — Thompson suggests Anthropic's partnership may be the quiet engine behind this — while Meta's AI spend still reads as speculative. A critical signal for how markets are now pricing the AI capex cycle.

2. **[Amazon's Durability](https://stratechery.com/2026/amazons-durability/)** — *Stratechery, May 5*
Thompson argues Amazon was underestimated during the training-centric era but is structurally advantaged for the inference era, where scale, infrastructure, and distribution matter most. AWS's long-term infrastructure bets and Bedrock's model-agnostic approach position it as the picks-and-shovels winner as AI workloads shift toward serving rather than building models.

3. **[The Distillation Panic](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/the-distillation-panic)** — *Interconnects, May 4*
Nathan Lambert pushes back on the framing of "distillation attacks," arguing the term is alarmist and mischaracterizes what is a natural and well-understood part of the ML ecosystem. The piece is a useful corrective as frontier labs increasingly position distillation as an existential threat to justify closed model strategies.

4. **[Last Week in AI #340](https://lastweekin.ai/p/last-week-in-ai-340-openai-vs-musk)** — *Last Week in AI, May 5*
A packed week: the Musk v. Altman trial is underway, OpenAI resolved its legal exposure with Microsoft over the $50B Amazon deal, and DeepSeek previewed V4 with claims of closing the gap to frontier models. The OpenAI corporate saga continues to absorb outsized attention relative to the actual technical developments.

5. **[LWiAI Podcast #243 — GPT 5.5, DeepSeek V4, AI Safety Sabotage](https://lastweekin.ai/p/lwiai-podcast-243-gpt-55-deepseek)** — *Last Week in AI, May 4*
Discussion covers the GPT-5.5 rollout, DeepSeek V4's benchmark performance, and emerging concerns around AI safety sabotage — models being deliberately prompted or fine-tuned to circumvent safety guardrails. The safety sabotage thread is worth watching as a potential regulatory catalyst.

## Takeaway
The most significant signal this cycle is **Google's AI monetization narrative winning with investors while Meta's does not** — it suggests the market has moved past rewarding AI ambition and is now demanding AI revenue, a phase change that will reshape which companies can sustain massive infrastructure spending and which face pressure to pull back.
