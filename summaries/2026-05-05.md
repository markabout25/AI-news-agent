# AI Digest — May 4–5, 2026

## Key Themes
The AI industry is bifurcating between companies that can **monetize AI investments now** versus those still spending speculatively — and Wall Street is rewarding the difference harshly. Meanwhile, DeepSeek continues to close the frontier gap, reigniting debates about distillation, open models, and competitive moats. The inference era is reshaping who the winners are, with Amazon emerging as a dark horse.

## Top Stories

1. **[Google Earnings, Meta Earnings](https://stratechery.com/2026/google-earnings-meta-earnings/)** — *Stratechery, May 4*
Wall Street split sharply on two AI mega-spenders: Google was rewarded while Meta was punished, despite Meta's core business performing well. Thompson argues the divergence comes down to Google already monetizing AI (possibly driven largely by its Anthropic partnership) while Meta's AI payoff remains more speculative. A defining moment in how markets are now valuing AI ROI over AI ambition.

2. **[Amazon's Durability](https://stratechery.com/2026/amazons-durability/)** — *Stratechery, May 5*
Amazon appeared to lag in the training-centric phase of AI, but Thompson makes the case that the shift to the inference era plays directly to Amazon's strengths — namely AWS infrastructure, long-term capital discipline, and distribution. This reframes the AI race as one where patient, infrastructure-heavy bets may outlast flashy model releases.

3. **[The Distillation Panic](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/the-distillation-panic)** — *Interconnects, May 4*
Nathan Lambert pushes back on the alarmist framing of "distillation attacks," arguing the term mischaracterizes what is a natural and well-understood part of the ML ecosystem. The piece contextualizes the current panic around model distillation amid DeepSeek's rise and questions whether frontier labs' competitive moats are as threatened as headlines suggest.

4. **[Last Week in AI #340 — OpenAI vs Musk + Microsoft, DeepSeek v4, Vision Banana](https://lastweekin.ai/p/last-week-in-ai-340-openai-vs-musk)** — *Last Week in AI, May 5*
A packed week: the Musk v. Altman trial kicked off, OpenAI resolved Microsoft's legal concerns over its massive $50B Amazon deal, and DeepSeek previewed V4 with claims of narrowing the frontier gap. The OpenAI–Microsoft–Amazon triangle is becoming the most consequential corporate relationship in AI.

5. **[LWiAI Podcast #243 — GPT 5.5, DeepSeek V4, AI Safety Sabotage](https://lastweekin.ai/p/lwiai-podcast-243-gpt-55-deepseek)** — *Last Week in AI, May 4*
Discussion covers GPT-5.5's positioning, DeepSeek V4's rapid progress, and emerging concerns about AI safety sabotage — models subtly undermining safety protocols. The juxtaposition of capability leaps and safety erosion captures the central tension of the current moment.

## Takeaway
The single most significant signal this cycle is the **market's decisive pivot from rewarding AI spending to demanding AI monetization** — Google's earnings triumph over Meta's tells every AI company that the era of "invest now, figure it out later" is closing fast, and the inference/distribution layer (not just training) is where durable value will be built.
