The open-source model ecosystem continues to mature rapidly, with multiple organizations shipping frontier-competitive models that push the Pareto frontier of capability vs. cost. Meanwhile, the major labs are in a fierce release cadence — Anthropic's Opus 5, Google's Gemini 3.6, and Moonshot's Kimi K3 all dropped in the same window — while Meta's earnings reveal growing investor anxiety about when massive AI capex will translate into revenue.

## Takeaway
The most significant signal this cycle is the **simultaneous arrival of Opus 5, Gemini 3.6, and a wave of competitive open models** — capability is proliferating faster than any single company can monetize it, which is exactly the tension Meta's earnings call laid bare. The strategic question is shifting from "who can build the best model" to "who can capture value before parity erases their edge."
